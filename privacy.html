Privacy Policy

Clip Studio · Last updated: September 21, 2026

Clip Studio is a personal desktop application used only by its owner to publish short video clips to the owner's own social media accounts. This policy explains what data it handles.

YouTube API Services

Clip Studio uses YouTube API Services to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. By using Clip Studio's YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

  • Google/YouTube: an OAuth sign-in token that permits uploading videos (youtube.upload), and the connected channel's name (youtube.readonly). No other YouTube data is read, collected, or modified.
  • Instagram (Meta): an access token that permits publishing Reels to the owner's own professional account, plus that account's username and ID.
  • Videos and captions that the owner creates and approves inside the app.

How data is used, stored, and shared

  • Tokens are used only to publish videos that the owner has manually approved and chosen to upload, and to show which account is connected.
  • All data, including tokens, is stored only on the owner's own computer. Clip Studio has no servers and no database, and it does not collect analytics.
  • Data is sent only directly to the platform being posted to (for example, YouTube). It is never sold, shared with, or disclosed to any other party.
  • No data from other users is ever accessed. Clip Studio has no other users.

Retention and deletion

  • Tokens remain on the owner's computer until the owner disconnects the account or deletes the app's secrets folder, which removes them immediately.
  • You can revoke Clip Studio's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • Instagram access can be removed from Instagram or Facebook under Settings → Apps and websites.

Cookies

Clip Studio does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies. This website does not either.
